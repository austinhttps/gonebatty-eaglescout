# gonebatty-eaglescout: Eagle Scout Service Project Portfolio

Eagle Scout Service Project: Wildlife Habitat &amp; Conservation Initiative ("Gone Batty")

A clean, modern, and personal one-page portfolio website designed to showcase an Eagle Scout Service Project. It presents general information about the project in a straightforward, authentic format suitable for friends, family, troop members, and the Eagle Scout Board of Review.

---

## 🌲 Design & Colors
- **Forest Green** (`#1B3D2B`) — Outdoors, trail, and forestry theme.
- **Navy Blue** (`#162846`) — Traditional scouting blue.
- **Charcoal Gray** (`#1F2421`, `#2F353B`) — Clean, modern, highly readable body copy.
- **Crisp White & Off-White** (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAF8`) — Clean, balanced spacing.
- **Typography** — **Plus Jakarta Sans** (bold modern headings) & **Inter** (clean body text).

---

## 📑 Single-Page Structure

1. **Hero Section (`#home`)**: Large project header image with bold text overlay ("Eagle Scout Service Project Portfolio"), project title, beneficiary name, and quick candidate details.
2. **The Mission (`#project`)**: Two-column layout. Left side covers the community need, project goal, and beneficiary. Right side displays a "Before" site photo.
3. **Leadership & Impact Stats (`#impact`)**: 4 clean visual cards showing:
   - Total Volunteer Hours (e.g. `248` hrs)
   - Funds Raised & Budget Managed (e.g. `$4,320`)
   - Total Volunteers Led (e.g. `36` people)
   - Project Timeline (e.g. `6` months)
4. **Work Log & Phases (`#timeline`)**: Step-by-step summary of the 4 project phases:
   - Proposal & Approvals
   - Fundraising & Materials
   - Execution Workdays
   - Final Inspection & Handover
5. **Before & After Gallery (`#gallery`)**: 4-card responsive photo grid highlighting the initial condition, build workdays, volunteer leadership, and the completed build.
6. **Leadership Reflection (`#reflection`)**: Honest, personal reflections on directing volunteers rather than doing all the manual work, solving unexpected on-site challenges, and collaborating with community partners.
7. **Contact & Footer (`#contact`)**: Simple contact card with candidate name, troop number, council, and email link, followed by a clean footer.

---

## ✏️ Customization

Open `index.html` in any text editor to edit:
- **Your Name & Troop Info**: Search for `Austin [Last Name]`, `Troop 42`, and `Great Trail Council`.
- **Project Name & Beneficiary**: In the Hero section and Mission section.
- **Stats**: Edit the numbers in `<div class="stat-value">`.
- **Photos**: Update the `<img src="..." />` tags with URLs or paths to your own project photos.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

No installations required. Simply double-click `index.html` to view it in your web browser.
